# PROJECT: AFTERLIGHT - Game Design Document

## 1. Game Concept
**Title:** Project: Afterlight
**Genre:** Open-World Third-Person Action-Adventure
**Target Audience:** Mature (M), fans of narrative-driven action games, open-world exploration, and tactical combat.
**Tone:** Cinematic, grounded, neo-noir, mysterious, high-stakes.

**Core Premise:**
Set in the sprawling, rain-slicked coastal metropolis of "Oakhaven," you play as Elias Thorne, a former corporate fixer whose memory was partially wiped after a job gone wrong. The city is controlled by three massive factions. Elias must navigate the city's criminal underbelly, utilize an arsenal of conventional and experimental weaponry, and uncover the truth behind "The Afterlight"—a classified blackout event that plunged parts of the city into a permanent, quarantine-enforced night.

## 2. Core Design Principles
1.  **Agency & Consequence:** Combat and stealth are equally viable. Factions react to who you hit and who you help.
2.  **Tactical Gunplay & Brutal Melee:** Combat relies on positioning, exploiting cover, and fluidly switching between ranged and close-quarters combat.
3.  **Seamless World:** Zero loading screens during open-world traversal. Interiors and exteriors flow naturally.
4.  **Meaningful Exploration:** No "map vomit" (useless collectibles). Every hidden cache or data log reveals lore, provides unique gear, or opens new side missions.
5.  **Dynamic World Systems:** Weather, time of day, and faction territory control dynamically alter enemy patrols and available events.

## 3. World Design: Oakhaven
Oakhaven is a dense, interconnected map divided into distinct districts:
*   **The Neon Grid (Downtown):** High-rises, corporate HQs, dense traffic, heavily policed.
*   **The Dross (Industrial):** Abandoned factories, scrapyards, gang-controlled.
*   **Silverside (Residential/Suburban):** Quiet neighborhoods hiding dark secrets, wealthy estates.
*   **The Blackout Zone (Quarantine):** The epicenter of the Afterlight event. Perpetual darkness, highly dangerous, high-tier loot, mutated/experimental enemies.
*   **The Weave (Underground):** Sewers, subway tunnels, smuggler routes connecting the city.

## 4. Characters & Factions
**Protagonist:** Elias Thorne. Pragmatic, highly skilled, searching for his identity and leverage.
**Factions:**
*   **Aegis Security (Law/Corporate):** Heavily armored, use drones, tactical formations.
*   **The Rust Syndicate (Organized Crime):** Numerous, aggressive, use modified civilian vehicles and heavy weaponry.
*   **The Null (Cult/Tech Extremists):** Stealthy, use EMP tech, agile, primarily found in the Blackout Zone.

## 5. Gameplay Loop
**Explore** the districts -> **Discover** faction outposts, hidden caches, or dynamic events -> **Engage** using stealth or combat -> **Earn** credits, reputation, and salvage -> **Upgrade** weapons, vehicles, and skills at Safehouses -> **Unlock** new story missions and traversal tools -> **Explore Deeper**.

## 6. Story Overview
*   **Act 1:** Elias wakes up in the Dross. He rebuilds his basic arsenal and establishes a safehouse while dealing with local Rust Syndicate thugs. He discovers he is being hunted by Aegis.
*   **Act 2:** Elias pits the factions against each other to gain access to the Neon Grid's corporate servers. He learns about the "Afterlight" project.
*   **Act 3:** The factions go to open war. Elias breaches the Blackout Zone to confront the architects of his memory wipe.
*   **Act 4:** A massive assault on the Aegis headquarters.
*   **Act 5:** The truth is revealed; Elias makes a world-altering choice regarding the city's power grid and control.

## 7. Mission Structure
Missions utilize a modular framework:
*   **Infiltration:** Breach an area undetected.
*   **Assault:** Clear an area of hostiles.
*   **Extraction:** Rescue a VIP or steal data and escape pursuit.
*   **Investigation:** Use scanner mechanics to track targets or solve environmental puzzles.
*   **Set-Pieces:** High-octane chases or boss encounters.

## 8. Economy & Progression
*   **Currencies:** Credits (standard economy), Data-Cores (skill points), Salvage (weapon upgrades).
*   **Skill Trees:**
    *   *Ghost (Stealth & Hacking)*
    *   *Vanguard (Assault & Survivability)*
    *   *Drifter (Vehicle & Traversal)*
    *   *Specter (Marksman & Gadgets)*
